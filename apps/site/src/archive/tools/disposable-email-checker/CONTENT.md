## FAQ

**H2**  
FAQ

**What is a disposable email?**  
A disposable email is a temporary inbox people use instead of a real address.

**Why block disposable domains?**  
They’re commonly used for fake signups, spam, and free-trial abuse.

**Is domain checking enough?**  
It’s a strong first filter, but not the full picture. For better accuracy, combine it with IP and device checks.

**Can I check a full email address?**  
This page is built for domain lookup. Enter the domain only, like `mailinator.com`.
