Write a product landing page for Gatekeepr using the brief below.

COMPANY
Gatekeepr is a developer-first API for protecting signup flows. It analyzes email, IP, and user agent at signup and returns a final status of allow, challenge, or block. It also returns threats, trust signals, blocklists, and info so teams can understand why the decision happened.

Email Intelligence

- It flags suspicious plus-tag usage

- Do not talk about fraud scores or numeric scoring

SECTION NOTES
- The “validation vs intelligence vs signup protection” section is important. Explain:
  - email validation = syntax and basic domain checks
  - email intelligence = suspicious patterns, disposable domains, risky email behavior
  - signup protection = email plus IP and user agent together
- “What Gatekeepr checks inside an email” should cover disposable domains, role accounts, suspicious tags, separator abuse, composition oddities, and domain/MX-related context.
- “Examples of risky email patterns” should use concrete but simple examples like disposable inboxes, admin@ role accounts, high-randomness locals, or excessive separators.
- “How email signals influence allow / challenge / block” should show that not every risky email must be blocked; some should be challenged.
