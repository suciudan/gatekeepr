Write a product landing page for Gatekeepr using the brief below.

COMPANY
Gatekeepr is a developer-first API for protecting signup flows. It analyzes email, IP, and user agent at signup and returns a final status of allow, challenge, or block. It also returns threats, trust signals, blocklists, and info so teams can understand why the decision happened.

SECTION NOTES
- “Why free-trial abuse hurts SaaS economics” should mention wasted free credits, compute costs, support time, skewed activation/conversion metrics, and lower trust in top-of-funnel data.
- “Common abuse patterns” should mention disposable emails, repeat signups, datacenter/VPN/Tor traffic, suspicious email structure, and multi-account behavior.
- “How Gatekeepr decides allow / challenge / block” should make the product feel operational, not theoretical.
- “Example enforcement policies” should be concrete:

  - block = reject signup
