\# About Gatekeepr



Gatekeepr protects SaaS signup flows from fake accounts, disposable emails, and free-trial abuse.



We analyze email, IP, and device signals during signup and return a clear decision: \*\*allow, challenge, or block\*\*.



\## Why we built it



Most abuse starts before billing.



Fake signups pollute analytics, waste support time, and burn through free trials. Gatekeepr is built to stop that at the signup step.



\## What Gatekeepr does



Gatekeepr helps teams:



\-   block disposable and low-quality emails

\-   detect suspicious signup behavior

\-   reduce bot-driven account creation

\-   prevent repeated free-trial abuse



\## Built for SaaS teams



Most fraud tools are too broad, too heavy, or built for payments.



Gatekeepr is focused on one problem: protecting the front door of your product.



\## Built for developers



Fast to integrate. Easy to reason about. Designed for real signup flows.



No vague scores. Just actionable decisions your product can use immediately.



\## Our mission



Make signup abuse prevention simple for every SaaS team.
