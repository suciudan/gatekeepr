# Gatekeepr vs UserCheck

Short answer: as a product, no. As a core detection engine, sometimes yes.

UserCheck is better today if you judge by what a customer can trust and buy right now: sharper positioning, visible proof, real pricing, integrations, and a more complete commercial surface. Gatekeepr's stronger part is the underlying idea and technical scope: it goes beyond disposable-email checks into a broader signup-risk engine.

## Where Gatekeepr shines

- It is materially broader. The engine evaluates email, domain, IP, and user agent in one pass, not just email/domain reputation. That shows up in `apps/api/src/libs/checks.js`, `apps/api/src/checks/ip/provider.js`, `apps/api/src/checks/ip/tor.js`, and `apps/api/src/checks/ip/spamhaus.js`.
- The score is explainable. Gatekeepr exposes triggered threats/trust and computes a rule-based fraud score in `apps/api/src/actions/process.js` and `apps/api/src/config/score.js`. That is a better foundation for decisioning than a black-box disposable check.
- The product idea is more extensible. Cron-fed intelligence, ASN checks, TOR checks, cloud provider checks, domain age, MX, and UA analysis give it a path toward a real abuse-prevention platform, not just a blocklist API.
- If open-sourced, Gatekeepr has more builder appeal than UserCheck because the engine is understandable and hackable.

## Where Gatekeepr sucks

- The positioning is weaker. The homepage says "Block Fake Users in Seconds" and "Analyze email, IP, and user agent," but it does not tell one concrete buyer exactly what pain disappears first. See `apps/site/src/app/page.jsx`, `apps/site/src/components/HowItWorks.jsx`, and `apps/site/src/components/Features.jsx`.
- The commercial surface is thin. Pricing is vague, upgrades are manual, and the CTA ends in a contact-style flow. `apps/site/src/components/FAQ.jsx` and `apps/site/src/components/Dashboard/UpgradeForm.jsx` make this obvious.
- Trust signals are much weaker. UserCheck shows customer logos, case studies, pricing tiers, uptime, request volume, and integrations on its homepage and docs:
  - https://www.usercheck.com/
  - https://www.usercheck.com/docs/get-started/overview
  - https://www.usercheck.com/docs/integrations/laravel
  - https://www.usercheck.com/docs/integrations/zapier
- The product completeness is behind. UserCheck already advertises environments, webhooks, bulk validation, custom blocklists, and no-code or SDK-style integrations. Gatekeepr does not currently match that surface.
- Reliability and polish are still hurting the product. One API test currently fails, another test file is misnamed, and docs drift from runtime behavior. Relevant files: `apps/api/test/email.test.js`, `apps/docs/api/send-request.md`, and `apps/api/src/middlewares/ctxMiddleware.js`.

## Bottom line

- UserCheck is better as a sellable product today.
- Gatekeepr is better as a broader technical concept.
- If reliability is tightened and the product is repositioned around one concrete job, Gatekeepr can become more compelling than UserCheck for customers who want signup-risk scoring instead of only disposable-email filtering.
